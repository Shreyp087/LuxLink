During an outage, nearby places can become isolated even when people can still move. LuxLink turns
one browser into a human-carried offline relay. Write a short, time-bounded emergency message. A
local P-256 identity signs the exact bytes; no account or cloud API is involved. The packet becomes
paced QR frames with corruption checks and a SHA-256 transfer digest. A second device can scan them
by camera. For this hardware-free demo, Verify on this device uses the same reassembly, parser, hash,
and signature-verification pipeline. The receipt separates cryptographic integrity from identity
trust: a valid signature proves the bytes survived, not who the signer represents. The verified
message is stored locally and remains available offline. A carrier can append a signed custody hop
and relay it without changing the original message. LuxLink: when the network stops, the message
walks.
